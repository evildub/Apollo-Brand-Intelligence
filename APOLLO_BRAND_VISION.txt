1. THE CORE PROBLEM WITH ENTERPRISE LEGACY (THE GENESIS REALITY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The Titanic Syndrome: Legacy enterprise platforms (Genesis) pull in millions 
  of unfiltered, noisy listings due to crude, broad-brush automated queries.
  • Case in Point: On the Stellantis account for US and EMEA alone, over 2.7 MILLION 
    unaddressed results sit idling in a single marketplace backlog.
- Marketplace Syntax Blindness: Analysts and CSMs configure uniform keyword rules 
  expecting them to behave identically across all platforms. 
  • In reality, every marketplace requires distinct query operators (`-`, `+`, `NOT`, 
    exact quotes `""`). A search that works on eBay floods the queue with 10s-100s 
    of thousands of benign results on AliExpress, Vinted, or Temu.
  • Example: A search for Stellantis's luxury brand "DS" blindly harvests hundreds 
    of thousands of Nintendo DS video games and accessories, paralyzing the backlog.
- Brittle Syndicate Mapping: Existing legacy syndicate tools constantly break because 
  they rely on superficial string matches easily evaded by burner accounts.

4. UI QUALITY GATES & RELEASE PROTOCOL (OPERATIONAL HYGIENE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Window & Modal Standardization:
  - Every new tk.Toplevel modal MUST invoke `master._apply_dark_titlebar(self)` or 
    `parent._apply_dark_titlebar(self)` in `__init__`.
  - Must inherit background/panel colors from active theme (no raw unstyled white windows).
  - Must center dynamically relative to master or screen dimensions.

• Glyph & Emoji Spacing Rule:
  - Multi-codepoint emojis (e.g. 🛠️, 🛍️, ⚙️) require 2 spaces before label text 
    (e.g., "🛠️  ManoMano") to prevent Windows DirectWrite font clipping and text collision.

• Release & Deployment Discipline:
  - "Full send" strictly means compiling the standalone binary locally for Home Base 
    operational testing and verification.
  - NEVER commit or push to Git/GitHub releases without explicit user instruction.
